Isombe

East Africa · Rwanda

About the dish

Isombe is a Rwandan favourite made from finely pounded cassava leaves slow-cooked with eggplant, spinach, onion and groundnut paste. It is often served with ugali, rice, plantain or potatoes and is a staple at family meals and gatherings.

Ingredients

Servings 4
  • 500 g pounded cassava leaves (fresh or frozen)
  • 1 eggplant, diced
  • 1 onion and 3 garlic cloves, chopped
  • 2 tbsp palm or vegetable oil
  • 3 tbsp groundnut (peanut) paste
  • 1 green pepper, salt, 3 cups water

Instructions

Build the base
  1. Boil the cassava leaves in water for 20 minutes, then drain.
  2. Sauté onion, garlic, pepper and eggplant in the oil until soft.
Simmer
  1. Add the leaves and 3 cups of water; cook on low heat for 45–60 minutes.
  2. Stir in the groundnut paste and simmer 15 minutes until thick.
Serve

Taste for salt and serve hot with rice, ugali or boiled plantain.
