West Africa

Bold, peppery and communal. East Africa · North Africa

Food culture

West African cooking revolves around generous, shared pots and layers of heat. Meals are built on starchy staples like yam, cassava, rice and millet, paired with thick soups and stews of egusi (melon seed), okra or groundnut.

Staples

Rice, yam, plantain, black-eyed peas, palm oil, and fish or goat meat.

Techniques

Cooks fry the pepper base until the oil separates, pound starches like fufu in a mortar, and grill over open flame for suya and fish.

Example dishes

Tomato-pepper rice found at every celebration.
