East Africa

Highland grains, spice-route flavours and Indian Ocean influence. West Africa · North Africa

Food culture

From the Ethiopian highlands to Rwanda's green hills and the Swahili coast, East African food is shaped by farming, fasting traditions and centuries of spice trade. Eating together from a shared platter, sometimes by hand, is a sign of friendship.

Staples

Teff, maize (ugali), beans, bananas, cassava leaves, lentils and berbere spice blends.

Techniques

Fermenting batter for injera, slow-simmering leafy stews, and grilling meat as nyama choma.

Example dishes

Fermented teff flatbread from Ethiopia.

Recipe
