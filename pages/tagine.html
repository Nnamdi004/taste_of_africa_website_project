Chicken Tagine

North Africa · Morocco

About the dish

"Tagine" names both the cone-lidded clay pot and the slow-braised stew cooked inside it. This classic Moroccan version balances preserved lemon, green olives, saffron, ginger and cumin. The lid traps steam so the chicken turns tender with very little added liquid.

Ingredients

Servings 6
  • 6 bone-in chicken thighs
  • 2 onions, grated or finely sliced
  • 3 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tsp each ginger, turmeric, cumin; pinch of saffron
  • 1 preserved lemon, rind sliced
  • ¾ cup green olives
  • 2 tbsp olive oil, fresh coriander and parsley

Instructions

Marinate and brown
  1. Rub the chicken with the spices, garlic and olive oil; rest 30 minutes.
  2. Brown the pieces in the tagine or a heavy pot.
Braise
  1. Add the onions and ½ cup water, cover, and simmer on low for 40 minutes.
  2. Add the preserved lemon and olives and cook 10 minutes uncovered to thicken the sauce.
Serve

Scatter with herbs and serve with crusty bread or couscous.
