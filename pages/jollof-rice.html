Jollof Rice

West Africa · Nigeria

About the dish

Jollof is rice cooked in a spiced tomato and pepper sauce, and it is the centrepiece of weddings, birthdays and Sunday lunches across West Africa. Nigerians and Ghanaians famously debate whose version is best. Nigerian party jollof uses long-grain rice and a smoky tomato–pepper base with scotch bonnet, thyme and curry powder.

Ingredients

Servings 6
  • 3 cups long-grain parboiled rice
  • 5 ripe tomatoes, 2 red bell peppers, 2 scotch bonnets (blended; use fewer for milder heat)
  • 1 large onion, sliced
  • ½ cup vegetable oil, 2 tbsp tomato paste
  • 2 cups chicken or vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp each thyme and curry powder, 2 bay leaves
  • Salt to taste

Instructions

Make the sauce
  1. Heat the oil and fry the onion until soft.
  2. Stir in the tomato paste for 3–4 minutes, then add the blended peppers.
  3. Cook uncovered until the sauce reduces and the oil floats on top.
Cook the rice
  1. Add stock, thyme, curry powder, bay leaves and salt.
  2. Wash the rice until the water runs clear, then stir it in.
  3. Cover tightly (use foil under the lid) and cook on low heat for 25–30 minutes.
Finish and serve

Fluff with a fork and serve with fried plantain, chicken or moin-moin.
