About Us

Our mission

Tastes of Africa exists to celebrate and preserve African food heritage, sharing the recipes, ingredients and stories that connect families and communities across the continent and the diaspora.

A brief history of African cuisine

African food traditions stretch back thousands of years. Indigenous crops such as sorghum, millet, yams and teff were cultivated long before the arrival of tomatoes, chillies and maize from the Americas, which were later folded into local cooking.

Trade routes across the Sahara, the Nile and the Indian Ocean carried spices, grains and techniques between regions. Today African flavours travel the world: jollof, injera and tagine appear on menus far beyond their homelands, and African chefs are leading a new wave of global interest.

A cultural food landmark

Jemaa el-Fnaa in Marrakech, Morocco, transforms every evening into one of the world's great open-air food markets.
